Accessing the Forms Page

All forms in GoHighLevel are managed through the Sites section. Follow these steps to access the forms page.

1 Navigate to Sites

In the left sidebar, click on "Sites" to expand the menu.

2 Select Forms

Click on "Forms" from the dropdown menu to view all existing forms in your account.

Screenshot: Forms page showing all existing forms
💡 Pro Tip: You can use the search bar to quickly find a specific form by name.
🔀

Creating a Form - Two Options

There are two ways to create a form in GoHighLevel. Choose the method that best fits your needs.

A Duplicate an Existing Form

Best when you want to reuse the structure and fields of an existing form. This saves time and ensures consistency.

B Create a New Form

Best when you need a completely new form with different fields, or want to start from a template.

Screenshot: Form creation buttons - Duplicate or Create New
📋

Option A: Duplicating an Existing Form

Duplicating a form is the fastest way to create a new form based on an existing one. All fields, settings, and styling will be copied.

1 Find the Form to Duplicate

Locate the form you want to duplicate from the forms list.

2 Click the Duplicate Option

Click on the three-dot menu or duplicate icon next to the form, then select "Duplicate" or "Clone".

Screenshot: Selecting the duplicate option for a form
3 Name Your Duplicated Form

Enter a new name for your duplicated form. Use a clear, descriptive name that identifies its purpose.

4 Edit as Needed

The duplicated form will open in the editor. Make any necessary changes to fields, labels, or settings.

Screenshot: A duplicated form ready for editing
💡 Best Practice: When duplicating forms, always rename them immediately to avoid confusion with the original form.
✨

Option B: Creating a New Form

Create a brand new form from scratch or use a template as your starting point.

1 Click "Create New" or "+ New Form"

From the forms page, click the button to create a new form.

Screenshot: Creating a new form
2 Choose a Template (Optional)

You can select from pre-built templates or start with a blank form. Templates provide a quick starting point with common fields already set up.

Screenshot: Choosing a form template
3 Name Your Form

Give your form a clear, descriptive name that identifies its purpose.

4 Add and Configure Fields

Add the fields you need (name, email, phone, custom fields, etc.) and configure their settings.

💡 Pro Tip: Start with a template if you're new to form building - it's easier to remove fields you don't need than to build everything from scratch.
🔧

Form Anatomy & Field Settings

Understanding the structure of a form and how to configure field settings is essential for creating effective forms.

1 Understanding Form Anatomy

A form consists of several key components: header/title, form fields, labels, placeholders, validation rules, and submit button.

Screenshot: Form anatomy - understanding the different parts of a form
2 Configuring Field Settings

Click on any field to access its settings. You can configure:

  • Field label and placeholder text
  • Required vs optional
  • Field type (text, email, phone, dropdown, etc.)
  • Validation rules
  • Custom field mapping
Screenshot: Field settings panel for configuring individual form fields
3 Save Your Form

Once you've configured all fields, click "Save" to save your form. You can then embed it on a page or share the direct link.

⚠️ Important: Always test your form after creating it to ensure all fields work correctly and submissions are being captured properly.
