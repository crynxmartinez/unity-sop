Accessing the Funnels Page

All funnels in GoHighLevel are managed through the Sites section. Follow these steps to access the funnels page.

1 Navigate to Sites

In the left sidebar, click on "Sites" to expand the menu.

2 Select Funnels

Click on "Funnels" from the dropdown menu to view all existing funnels in your account.

Screenshot: Funnels page showing all existing funnels
💡 Pro Tip: You can use the search bar and filters to quickly find a specific funnel by name or status.
🔀

Creating a Funnel - Options

There are multiple ways to create a funnel in GoHighLevel. Choose the method that best fits your needs.

1 Duplicate an Existing Funnel

Best when you want to reuse the structure and pages of an existing funnel. This saves time and ensures consistency.

Screenshot: Duplicating a funnel and choosing a name
2 Create a New Funnel

Click "+ New Funnel" to see all available options for creating a new funnel from scratch.

Screenshot: New funnel creation options
💡 Best Practice: If you're new to funnel building, start by duplicating an existing funnel or using a template to learn the structure.
✨

Using Templates or AI

GoHighLevel offers pre-built templates and AI-powered funnel creation to help you get started quickly.

1 Browse the Templates Library

Access a wide variety of pre-designed funnel templates for different purposes - lead generation, sales, webinars, and more.

Screenshot: Templates library with various funnel templates
2 Use AI to Generate a Funnel

Let AI create a funnel for you based on your requirements. Simply describe what you need and AI will generate the structure.

Screenshot: Using AI to create a funnel
💡 Pro Tip: Templates are great starting points - you can always customize them after importing to match your brand and needs.
📋

Inside the Funnel - Creating Steps

Once you've created a funnel, you'll need to understand its structure and how to add steps (pages) to it.

1 Understanding the Funnel Structure

A funnel consists of multiple steps, each representing a page in your customer journey (landing page, opt-in, thank you page, etc.).

Screenshot: Inside a funnel showing the step structure
2 Adding New Steps

Click "+ Add New Step" to add additional pages to your funnel. Each step can be a different type of page.

Screenshot: Creating and managing funnel steps
⚠️ Important: Plan your funnel flow before adding steps. Think about the customer journey from start to finish.
🎨

Building Funnel Pages

Each step in your funnel needs a page. You can build pages from scratch, use templates, or import from other sources.

1 Edit the Step Page

Click on a step to open the page editor. Here you can design and customize your page.

Screenshot: Editing a funnel step page
2 Import from Different Sources

You can import pages from templates, other funnels, or external sources to speed up your workflow.

Screenshot: Importing pages from different sources
3 Create and Customize the Page

Use the drag-and-drop editor to add elements, customize styling, and build your page exactly how you want it.

Screenshot: Creating and customizing the funnel page
4 Save and Publish

Once your page is ready, save your changes and publish the funnel to make it live.

💡 Best Practice: Always preview your funnel on both desktop and mobile before publishing to ensure it looks great on all devices.
